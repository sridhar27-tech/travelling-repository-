# Mysore (Mysuru) - The Cultural Capital of Karnataka

## Overview
**Mysore**, officially known as **Mysuru**, is one of the most culturally rich cities in the Indian state of **Karnataka**. It is famously known as the **City of Palaces**, **Cultural Capital of Karnataka**, and the former capital of the **Kingdom of Mysore**.

Mysore is renowned for its royal heritage, magnificent palaces, beautiful gardens, and vibrant festivals — especially **Dasara**.

## Major Tourist Attractions

### Palaces & Historical Sites
- **Mysore Palace (Amba Vilas Palace)** – One of the most magnificent palaces in India
- **Chamundi Hills** – With giant Nandi statue and Chamundeshwari Temple
- **Lalitha Mahal Palace** – Now a heritage hotel
- **Jayalakshmi Vilas Mansion**
- **Mysore Zoo** – One of the oldest and best zoos in India
- **St. Philomena’s Cathedral** – Beautiful Neo-Gothic architecture

### Gardens & Parks
- **Brindavan Gardens** – Famous musical fountain and terraced gardens
- **Karanji Lake** – Beautiful lake with butterfly park
- **Cubbon Park** (wait, that's Bangalore) – Wait, in Mysore: **Manas Gangotri Botanical Garden**

### Other Attractions
- **Srirangapatna** – Tipu Sultan’s capital (nearby)
- **Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary**
- **Shuka Vana** (Parrot Sanctuary)
- **Railway Museum**
- **Folklore Museum**

---

## Famous Festivals
- **Dasara (Dussehra)** – Grandest festival of Mysore with grand procession, lights, and cultural programs (September/October)
- **Mysuru Dasara** is a UNESCO-listed intangible cultural heritage candidate

---

## Cuisine of Mysore
Mysore is famous for authentic **Karnataka cuisine**:
- Mysore Masala Dosa
- Mysore Pak (famous sweet)
- Bisi Bele Bath
- Ragi Mudde
- Filter Coffee
- Jolada Roti with Enne Badnekayi

Popular eateries: Hotel Dasaprakash, Vinayaka Mylari, Hotel Roopa

---

## Economy & Industry
- Known for **Sandalwood**, Silk, and Incense sticks
- Major producer of **Mysore Silk Sarees**
- Growing IT and Tourism industry
- Educational hub with University of Mysore (established 1916)

---

## Climate & Best Time to Visit
- Pleasant climate most of the year
- **Best Time**: October to March
- Ideal to visit during **Dasara festival** for the full royal experience

---

## Quick Facts
- Mysore was the capital of the **Wadiyar dynasty** for centuries.
- Home to the famous **University of Mysore** – one of the oldest universities in India.
- World-famous for **Mysore Painting** and traditional crafts.
- The city maintains a royal and heritage charm even today.
