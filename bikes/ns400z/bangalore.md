# Bangalore (Bengaluru) - The Silicon Valley of India

## Overview
**Bangalore**, officially known as **Bengaluru**, is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of **Karnataka**. It is one of the most important metropolitan cities in India and the **leading IT hub** of the country.

Popularly known as the **Silicon Valley of India**, Bangalore is famous for its pleasant climate, beautiful gardens, vibrant culture, and booming startup ecosystem.

## Historical Significance
Bangalore was founded in 1537 by Kempe Gowda I, a chieftain under the Vijayanagara Empire. It later came under the rule of the **Mysore Kingdom** and the **British Empire**. The city earned the nickname **Garden City** due to its lush greenery and pleasant weather.

---

## Major Tourist Attractions

### Historical & Cultural Sites
- **Bangalore Palace** – Inspired by Windsor Castle
- **Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace**
- **Lalbagh Botanical Garden** – Famous for its glasshouse and flower shows
- **Cubbon Park** – Beautiful 120-acre park in the heart of the city
- **Vidhana Soudha** – Seat of the Karnataka State Legislature
- **ISKCON Temple**
- **Bull Temple (Nandi Temple)**

### Modern Attractions
- **MG Road & Brigade Road** – Major shopping and entertainment hubs
- **UB City Mall**
- **Bannerghatta National Park** – Wildlife safari and butterfly park
- **Wonderla Water Park**
- **Innovative Film City**
- **Snow World**

### Lakes
- Ulsoor Lake
- Madiwala Lake
- Hebbal Lake
- Sankey Tank

## Economy & IT Industry
- Global leader in **Information Technology** and **Startup Ecosystem**
- Home to headquarters and major offices of: Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Intel, etc.
- Known as the **Startup Capital of India**
- Major contributor to India’s software exports

---

## Cuisine of Bangalore
Bangalore offers a rich variety of food:
- **Karnataka Cuisine**: Bisi Bele Bath, Ragi Mudde, Jolada Roti, Mysore Masala Dosa
- **Street Food**: Vada Pav, Pani Puri, Gobi Manchurian
- **Filter Coffee** – World-famous South Indian filter coffee
- Multi-cuisine restaurants and pubs on MG Road, Koramangala, and Indiranagar

---

## Climate & Best Time to Visit
- Known for its **pleasant weather** throughout the year
- **Best Time**: October to February (Winter & pleasant season)
- Mild summers and moderate rainfall

## Culture & Festivals
- Celebrates **Karnataka Rajyotsava** (State Day) with great enthusiasm
- Major festivals: Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali, Ugadi, Dasara, Christmas
- Vibrant pub culture and nightlife, especially in areas like MG Road, Koramangala, and Whitefield
