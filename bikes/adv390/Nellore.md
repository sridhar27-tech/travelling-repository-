# Nellore - The Rice Bowl of Andhra Pradesh

## Overview
**Nellore** is a major city and district headquarters in the southern part of **Andhra Pradesh**. It is famously known as the **"Rice Bowl of Andhra Pradesh"** due to its extensive rice cultivation and agricultural economy.

Nellore is located on the banks of the Penna River and is an important commercial and educational center.

## Economy
- Leading producer of **Rice** and **Shrimp** (Aquaculture)
- Mica mining
- Growing industrial sector
- Educational hub with many engineering colleges
