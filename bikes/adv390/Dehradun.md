# Dehradun - The Capital of Uttarakhand

## Overview
**Dehradun** is the capital city of **Uttarakhand** and is famously known as the **"Gateway to Garhwal Himalayas"**. Located in the Doon Valley between the Himalayas and the Shivalik ranges, it offers a pleasant climate and scenic beauty.

Dehradun is a major educational hub, a popular tourist destination, and an important administrative center in North India.

## Major Tourist Attractions

### Nature & Scenic Spots
- **Robber's Cave (Guchhupani)** – Natural cave with stream
- **Sahastradhara** – Sulphur springs and waterfall
- **Tapkeshwar Temple** – Ancient cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva
- **Mindrolling Monastery** – One of the largest Buddhist centers in India
- **Forest Research Institute (FRI)** – Beautiful colonial architecture and museum
- **Malsi Deer Park**
- **Lachhiwala** – Natural swimming pools in the forest

### Nearby Attractions
- **Mussoorie** (Queen of Hills) – 35 km
- **Doon Valley**
- **Rajaji National Park**
- **Dakpathar**
- **Asan Barrage**

## Education Hub
Dehradun is known as the **"School Capital of India"** because of its prestigious educational institutions:
- The Doon School
- Welham Girls’ School
- Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC)
- Indian Military Academy (IMA)
- Forest Research Institute (FRI)
- Uttaranchal University, Graphic Era University, etc.

---

## Cuisine of Dehradun
- Famous for **Garhwali Cuisine**
- Must-try dishes:
  - Garhwali Thali
  - Chainsoo
  - Jhangora Ki Kheer
  - Bal Mithai
  - Momos & Tibetan food (due to Tibetan community)
- Popular street food: Chaat, Gol Gappe, Aloo Puri

---

## Climate & Best Time to Visit
- Pleasant weather throughout the year
- **Best Time**: March to June (Summer) and September to November (Post-Monsoon)
- Winters are cold but beautiful

---

## Economy & Industry
- Major hub for **Education**, **Tourism**, and **Defense**
- Growing IT and startup scene
- Known for **Basmati Rice**, **Litchi**, and **Tea**
- Home to many multinational companies and research institutes

---

## Quick Facts
- Dehradun is one of the cleanest cities in North India.
- The city is surrounded by **dense forests** and **fruit orchards**.
- Famous for **Clementine oranges** and high-quality **Basmati rice**.
- Hosts the **Indian Military Academy (IMA)** – one of the premier officer training academies in the world.
