# Andhra Pradesh - The Rice Bowl of India

## Overview
**Andhra Pradesh** is a southeastern state of India known for its rich cultural heritage, ancient temples, delicious cuisine, and long coastline. It is one of the largest rice-producing states in India.

---

## Key Information

| Attribute              | Details                                      |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Capital**            | Amaravati (Legislative)                      |
| **Largest City**       | Visakhapatnam (Vizag)                        |
| **Population**         | 5+ Crore                                     |
| **Languages**          | Telugu (Official), Urdu, English             |
| **Major Rivers**       | Godavari, Krishna, Penna                     |
| **Coastline**          | 974 km (2nd longest in India)                |

## Cuisine
World-famous **Andhra Cuisine**:
- Hyderabadi-style spicy food (influenced)
- Gongura, Avakaya (Mango Pickle)
- Pulihora, Biryani, Paya
- Seafood in coastal regions

---

## Economy
- Agriculture (Rice, Chillies, Tobacco)
- IT & Pharma (Vizag & Hyderabad region influence)
- Petrochemicals, Ports, and Tourism
