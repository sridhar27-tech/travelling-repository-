# Visakhapatnam (Vizag) - The City of Destiny

## Overview
**Visakhapatnam**, popularly known as **Vizag**, is the largest city and financial capital of **Andhra Pradesh**. It is a major port city on the eastern coast of India and is known for its beautiful beaches, industrial strength, and naval base.

Vizag is often called the **"City of Destiny"** and is one of the fastest-growing cities in India.

## Major Tourist Attractions

- **RK Beach** – Most popular beach in Vizag
- **Rushikonda Beach**
- **Kailasagiri Hill** – With giant Shiva-Parvati statue
- **INS Kurusa** – Submarine Museum
- **Araku Valley** (Nearby hill station)
- **Borra Caves**
- **Simhachalam Temple**
- **Yarada Beach**
- **Tenneti Park**
- **Ross Hill Church**

---

## Economy & Industry
- Major **Industrial & Port City**
- Home to Hindustan Shipyard, Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (Vizag Steel)
- Pharma City, IT & SEZ development
- Important **Indian Navy** base (Eastern Naval Command)
