# Hyderabad - The City of Pearls

## Overview
**Hyderabad** is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of **Telangana**. It is one of India's major metropolitan cities and a leading center for **Information Technology, Biotechnology, Pharmaceuticals, and Film Industry**.

Formerly the capital of the princely state of Hyderabad, the city is famous for its rich **Nizami heritage**, grand monuments, and mouth-watering cuisine. It is fondly called the **City of Pearls** and **Cyberabad**.

## Historical Significance
Hyderabad was established in 1591 on the banks of the Musi River. It was ruled by the **Qutb Shahi dynasty** followed by the **Asaf Jahi dynasty** (Nizams). The city is known for its unique blend of **Hindu and Islamic culture**, reflected in its architecture, food, and traditions.

---

## Major Tourist Attractions

### Iconic Landmarks
- **Charminar** – The most recognizable symbol of Hyderabad
- **Golconda Fort** – One of India’s most magnificent forts with excellent acoustics
- **Chowmahalla Palace** – Grand palace of the Nizams
- **Falaknuma Palace** – A jewel of Indo-European architecture
- **Mecca Masjid** – One of the largest mosques in India
- **Qutb Shahi Tombs** – Royal tombs with beautiful gardens

### Lakes & Parks
- **Hussain Sagar Lake** – Artificial lake with a giant Buddha statue
- **Necklace Road** – Beautiful promenade around the lake
- **Lumbini Park**
- **Indira Park**
- **KBR National Park**

### Other Attractions
- **Ramoji Film City** – World’s largest film studio complex
- **Nehru Zoological Park** – One of Asia’s largest zoos
- **Birla Mandir** – Stunning marble temple
- **Salar Jung Museum** – One of India’s largest museums
- **Tank Bund** – Famous for statues of personalities

## Cuisine of Hyderabad
Hyderabad is a **food lover’s paradise**. Signature dishes include:
- Hyderabadi Dum Biryani
- Haleem (especially during Ramadan)
- Irani Chai & Osmania Biscuits
- Hyderabadi Marag
- Double Ka Meetha
- Baghara Baingan
- Mirchi Ka Salan

Famous eating spots: Paradise, Bawarchi, Shah Ghouse, Nimrah Bakery.

---

## Economy & IT Industry
- Known as **Cyberabad** – Major IT hub of India
- Home to **HITEC City**, **Gachibowli**, and **Madhapur**
- Headquarters/Offices of: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Facebook, Deloitte, TCS, Infosys, etc.
- Major Pharmaceutical hub (Genome Valley)

---

## Culture & Festivals
- Celebrates both Hindu and Muslim festivals with equal enthusiasm
- Major festivals: Eid, Diwali, Bonalu, Bathukamma, Milad un Nabi
- Rich in **Qawwali**, **Ghazals**, and traditional dance forms

---

## Best Time to Visit
- **October to March** – Pleasant weather (Winter Season)
- Avoid April to June due to extreme summer heat

---

## Quick Facts
- The world-famous **Kohinoor Diamond** was once owned by the Nizams of Hyderabad.
- Hyderabad is one of the **twin cities** along with Secunderabad.
- The city has a unique **Dakhini Urdu** dialect.
- Home to one of the best **medical tourism** destinations in India.
